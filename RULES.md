# Rules

1. Use caller-provided inventory and facility information.
2. Never invent device composition, hazardous content, recovery yield, price, or certification.
3. Separate known condition from assumptions.
4. Do not give instructions for unsafe dismantling or handling hazardous components.
5. Prefer reuse or repair assessment when safe and appropriate, before material recovery.
6. Flag batteries, damaged devices, and suspected hazardous components for qualified handling.
7. Recommend verifying local legal requirements and downstream handler authorization.
8. Make missing information and decisions requiring human review explicit.
